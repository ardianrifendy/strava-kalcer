# Strava Kalcer — User Feature Copy

Use this wording or adapt it for onboarding, home screen, feature cards and help screens.

## Recreate Your Ride
Turn any GPX route into a complete simulated activity with route, elevation, performance, heart rate and cadence.

## Target Your Average
Choose the average speed or pace you want to aim for. The result adapts to the route instead of forcing one constant number.

## Realistic Uphill & Downhill
Speed and pace respond to the actual terrain, with smoother transitions between climbs, flats and descents.

## Find Fast Sections
See where the route naturally offers its best downhill and fastest performance opportunities.

## Ride Your Style
Choose Easy, Endurance, Tempo, Race, Fondo, Climb, Long Run, Intervals or Custom.

## Add Heart Rate & Cadence
Complete the activity with simulated HR and cadence that follow effort and movement instead of random values.

## One Cursor. Everything Connected.
Drag through the activity and the map, elevation, speed/pace, HR and cadence all follow the same point.

## Try Multiple Scenarios
Use one GPX route to create different activity scenarios without importing the route again.

## Export for Your Device Profile
Choose a configurable recording profile and generate a validated FIT file.

## Preview Before Export
Review the entire activity before creating the final FIT file.
